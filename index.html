About Me

I am a tourism lecturer and doctoral researcher at the University of Dundee, United Kingdom, with academic interests in tourism development, religious tourism, cultural heritage and community-based tourism.

My research examines the relationships between tourism, culture, religion, mobility and sustainability, with particular attention to tourist experiences and destination development.
